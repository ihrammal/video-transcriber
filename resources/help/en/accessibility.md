# Accessibility

## Screen readers
Every control has an accessible name and description. The status line repeats every message that is also shown, so a screen reader hears progress, results and errors without watching the window, and nothing is conveyed by colour alone.

## The Accessibility tab in Preferences
Show timestamps (Control plus Shift plus T) toggles the format of the transcript. High contrast, Larger focused controls, Simplified view, Announce progress and Sounds each change one thing and are remembered between runs. Accessibility settings (Control plus Shift plus A) gathers them in one dialog.

## Keyboard
In Preferences, Left/Right selects a tab while focus is on its tab label. Press Tab to enter its settings and Shift+Tab to move back. Space toggles a checkbox; arrows change a combo box or radio choice. Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab switch pages. The General checkbox “Check for updates automatically at startup” controls update notices.

Every menu item has a mnemonic, and the whole program can be driven from the keyboard. Control plus Slash opens the shortcut list, written in words.

## Interface language
Preferences offers English and Arabic. Control plus Shift plus 1 switches to English; Control plus Shift plus 2 switches to Arabic. The layout flips to right to left for Arabic.


## Text size and brief messages
Open Preferences with Ctrl+comma. General offers 100%, 125%, 150%, 175% and 200% text. The main window and Preferences scroll so keyboard focus can reach every control. Accessibility includes separate checkboxes for welcome and goodbye messages; each closes automatically within three seconds. Sound feedback also controls the greeting music.

Each greeting also has a checkbox to show it next time. You can change this choice later on the Accessibility tab without waiting for a greeting to appear.
