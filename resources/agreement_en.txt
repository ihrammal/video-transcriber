1. WHAT THE APPLICATION DOES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Vid-Trans is a desktop application for Windows that:
  a) converts the speech contained in a video file into timed captions
     (transcription) using an offline speech-recognition model;
  b) optionally translates those captions into another language;
  c) lets you edit the captions and export them as a standard .srt file or
     burn them into a new video file.

3. PRIVACY AND DATA HANDLING
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Transcription is performed locally on your computer. Your video file, its
audio track and the extracted captions are NOT uploaded to the author and
are NOT stored by the author.

When you press "Translate", the caption text is sent over the internet to a
third-party translation service in order to produce the translation. The
video and audio themselves are never sent. Translation therefore requires an
internet connection and involves the transmission of caption text to that
third party, whose own privacy policy applies to that transmission.

Temporary files (extracted audio, work data) are created in your operating
system temporary folder and are removed when you close the Application.
Application logs are stored locally in your user data folder and contain no
video content.

No personal identification data, telemetry, advertising identifiers or
analytics are collected by the Application.
